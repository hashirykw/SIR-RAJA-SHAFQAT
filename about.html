About

Sir Raja Shafqat Hayat

Sir Raja Shafqat Hayat teaches Cambridge O Level and AS & A Level Mathematics in Karachi. When Titan College announced he was joining its A Level Mathematics faculty in 2026, the college introduced him as one of the city’s most senior and respected maths teachers.

He studied at the University of Karachi. Alongside Titan College he teaches at Highbrow College and Heuser College, and he has taught at Credo College. Outside the classroom he runs the YouTube channel Math with Raja Shafqat, where he works through concepts, past papers and exam technique, and he recorded an online O Level Mathematics course for Orb-Ed.

His teaching is built around how Cambridge awards marks. A correct answer with no method shown can score less than a wrong answer with the right working, so every topic ends with the working written out in full and checked line by line.
